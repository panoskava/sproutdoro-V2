How focus sessions turn into living plants

Plant Lifecycle: Session → Seedling → Mature Plant

Key Rule: Each completed session plants ONE new seedling. The type of plant is random from your unlocked collection. The maturity level (1-5) is based on cumulative focus hours with that plant.

Mastery Progression

Each plant levels up based on cumulative focus time contributed to it:

  • Level 1 (Sprout): 0-60 min — tiny seedling
  • Level 2 (Growing): 60-180 min — small plant
  • Level 3 (Thriving): 180-300 min — medium plant
  • Level 4 (Mature): 300-480 min — full size
  • Level 5 (Blooming): 480+ min — flowering/glowing

The "Masterpiece" tag goes to the plant with the highest total focus time.
