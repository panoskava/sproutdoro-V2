Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Local-only data

Sproutdoro stores all your data locally in your browser using IndexedDB. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your personal information. Your focus sessions, garden plants, settings, and categories never leave your device unless you explicitly export them.

No cookies or analytics

We do not use cookies, third-party analytics, or tracking pixels. Onboarding and install preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage only.

Self-hosted fonts

Typography and icons are served from the app bundle. No requests are made to Google Fonts or other third-party font CDNs.

Browser notifications

If you enable notifications in Settings, Sproutdoro uses the browser's Notification API to alert you when sessions complete. Permission is requested only when you turn this on, and notifications are sent locally on your device.

Export and import

You can export your data as a JSON file and import it on another device or browser. You control where that file is stored.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub.
